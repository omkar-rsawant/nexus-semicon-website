About Us

Infrastructure specialists,
not generalists

NexusSemicon Infrastructure Solutions is a Pune-based consultancy building the managed EDA infrastructure layer that India's emerging semiconductor ecosystem needs but rarely finds.

Our founding team came out of EDA support, cloud infrastructure engineering, and semiconductor project management. We understood early that India's DLI-scheme chip-design startups needed something specific: a productised, compliance-ready, cloud-native verification stack — not a generic IT managed service that happens to run Linux.

Every deployment is OCI Mumbai-primary for cost efficiency, Ansible-managed for reproducibility, and DLI-audit-ready from day one. We operate on a simple principle: your engineers should think about RTL, not infrastructure.

EDA-first, not cloud-generic

Every architecture decision is made through the lens of EDA workloads — burst-heavy, license-constrained, compliance-sensitive. We don't adapt a generic MSP playbook; we built ours from scratch for semiconductor.

Cost efficiency, engineered in

DMTCP checkpoint-restart on OCI spot instances is our primary margin lever — and yours. We pass the 35–45% cloud cost advantage directly through to every client tier.

DLI compliance, automatic

India's semiconductor incentive programmes require consistent documentation. We generate, sign, and archive your DLI audit packs on schedule — so your incentive drawdown is never blocked by a missing report.

Code, not consoles

Our 40+ file Ansible codebase means your infrastructure is version-controlled, peer-reviewed, and reproducible. No manual steps, no snowflake servers, no one-person tribal knowledge.
